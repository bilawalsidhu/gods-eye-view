# Camera category acceptance captures

These screenshots and the 24-second recording were generated with
`scripts/qa-penndot-categories.mjs` against a production preview. The recording
is condensed to keep the complete toggle and selection sequence concise.

All camera records and green camera frames are synthetic test fixtures created
for this project and covered by its MIT licence. No PennDOT or other public
camera footage is included. Venue IDs test category membership without bundling
those venues' real feeds.

The globe uses Esri imagery with visible in-frame attribution; its provider terms
continue to apply. These captures document the app interface and do not grant
rights to third-party map imagery or trademarks.

- `cameras-general-only.png`: manually configured webcam fixtures in Cameras,
  with PennDOT Traffic Cams off.
- `penndot-traffic-cams.png`: PennDOT Traffic Cams on, Cameras off, with a selected
  traffic camera and the shared viewer.
- `penndot-camera-categories.mp4`: independent toggles, camera selection, release
  when a category turns off, and the shared viewer's OFF action.
