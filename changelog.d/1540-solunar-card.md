- A new SOLUNAR card in the right dock shows the next 7 days of hunting and
  fishing windows for a spot you click on the map (default: the middle of the
  map), in that spot's own time zone: major windows (moon overhead and
  underfoot) and minor windows (moonrise and moonset), sunrise, sunset, moon
  times, moon phase and illumination, and a 1-4 star day rating for hunting and
  fishing with the rule shown. A day with no moonrise or moonset says "none"
  instead of showing the next day's. It is computed in the browser; there is
  no outside data feed and no key. Checked against the US Naval Observatory
  within 2 minutes (Byrd-IT, #1540).
