- GNSS Integrity bins aircraft into H3 resolution-4 hexagons, the grid
  gpsjam.org uses, and draws each as its hexagon instead of a 0.5° square. As
  on gpsjam.org, every hexagon with an aircraft is shown: the three-aircraft
  minimum is gone, and discounting one bad aircraft remains the only
  small-sample guard. Cells are keyed by their H3 index and carry their centre
  and boundary.
