- Space Missions moves out of the Context panel into its own right-rail panel,
  with its own tab on phones, and becomes an ordinary layer. It no longer
  switches other layers off or refuses them while it runs, and Context leaves
  it and Satellites alone, so it runs beside Contacts in either order
  (Contacts no longer switches Satellites off). On desktop, opening Context or
  Space Missions collapses the other. Mission labels on the globe are
  clickable and select their mission, even where they sit over aircraft or
  ships, and picking a mission on the globe opens the panel. The Data Layers
  row gets previous and next mission controls. Share links keep the panel
  open or closed (a short link made from an older long link, which does not
  mention the panel, is two bytes longer), voice can open it, and Cockpit
  folds it away like the other map panels. An explicit user or voice
  Satellites off request made after Space Missions was switched on ends it
  and keeps Satellites off afterwards; scene, story, preset and other
  programmatic Satellites changes leave Space Missions running
  (manjunath22466, samehkhamis, #1826).
