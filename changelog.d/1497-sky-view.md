- New **Sky view**: a Sky button drops the camera to eye height on a chosen
  point and looks up, with the terrain horizon, satellites above you (live
  positions, "Overhead now", next-48 h passes with estimated brightness),
  8,920 stars, constellations and boundaries, the Sun, Moon and planets,
  a search box, point-at-the-sky on phones (WMM2025 declination), a time
  control with Sunset / Twilight end / Midnight / Moonrise / ISS-pass presets,
  a shareable `sv` link, and a Clear view eye button. Star data (HYG) is
  CC BY-SA 4.0 and the constellation data (Stellarium) is CC BY-SA, credited in
  the app and `DATA_SOURCES.md`.
