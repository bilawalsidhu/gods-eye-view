- Fold Surface Temperature into one Temperature layer with Land surface
  (default), Sea surface, Sea anomaly and Air (2 m) modes. The sea modes show
  daily GHRSST MUR25 temperature and its 2003-2014 anomaly from NASA GIBS, with
  exact palette readings at a clicked point and an optional Niño 3.4 box; the
  air mode shows NOAA GFS 2 m forecasts in °C or °F. Only the chosen mode draws,
  so two colour fields never stack (KnottyDyes, #1357).
