  ships, transit, local ADS-B, trains) selects it and centres it once, at
  every zoom; nothing is followed, and the selection stays while the map is
  dragged. Picks from the Context panel and search select the same way, and
  closing a card deselects its item. Following is the card's Follow, a
  spoken "track" or a share link (samehkhamis, #1938).
