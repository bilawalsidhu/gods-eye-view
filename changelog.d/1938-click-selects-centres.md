- A click on any moving thing (aircraft, military aircraft, satellites,
  ships, transit, local ADS-B, trains) selects it and centres it once, at
  every zoom; nothing is followed, and the selection stays while the map is
  dragged. A second click on the selected aircraft, military aircraft or
  satellite follows it, as its card's Follow does; a click on the followed
  one keeps following. Picks from the Context panel and search select the
  same way, and closing a card deselects its item. Following is a second
  click, the card's Follow, a spoken "track" or a share link
  (samehkhamis, #1938).
