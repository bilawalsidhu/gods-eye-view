- A click on any moving thing (aircraft, military aircraft, satellites,
  ships, transit, local ADS-B, trains) selects it and centres it once, at
  every zoom; nothing is followed, and the selection stays while the map is
  dragged. A second click on the selected aircraft, military aircraft,
  satellite or ship follows it, as its card's Follow does; a click on the
  followed one keeps following; the trail drawn up to the selected thing
  never takes that click from it. Ships can now be followed: the camera rides
  the ship where the vessel layer draws it, a ship's card offers Follow, a
  spoken "track" on a ship follows it, and a share link made while
  following one opens following it (a new thing field; links with a ship
  only selected, and every older link, still open selecting it). Picks
  from the Context panel and search select the same way, and closing a
  card deselects its item. Following is a second click, the card's
  Follow, a spoken "track" or a share link (samehkhamis, #1938).
