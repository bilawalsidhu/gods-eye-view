- A click on a submarine cable or landing point names it in a card on the map
  (CABLE · name, LANDING POINT · name), where the click met the line or at
  the reference of a pin or stem, and centres the camera there once at the
  same height, in place of a dive to 6.5 km that left the cable out of
  sight. The card stays until a click lands elsewhere, another layer selects
  something, or the layer is turned off (samehkhamis, #1959).
