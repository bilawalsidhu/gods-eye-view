- Vessels on the map are shaded sprites that show the view of the ship from
  where the camera is, instead of flat top-down hulls: each hull shape (the
  ten vessel kinds, a dredger and a pilot boat) is drawn from a sheet
  rendered from straight above down to the horizon, laid flat on the water
  along its course, so a ship seen at an oblique angle looks solid. A
  vessel keeps its kind's colour; aids to navigation keep their upright buoy,
  and a renderer without view sheets draws the flat hulls as before. A
  vessel's size now follows the camera's range: larger up close, smaller
  than an aircraft from far out, and shrinking and fading toward the
  horizon as aircraft do (Mazeyar Moeini Feizabadi, #0000).
