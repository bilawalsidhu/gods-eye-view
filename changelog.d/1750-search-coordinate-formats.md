- Search reads coordinates written the other common ways, with no geocoder
  or key: degrees, minutes and seconds (`40°26'46"N 79°58'56"W`), UTM
  (`18T 585628 4511322`), MGRS (`18T WL 85650 11369`) and full plus codes
  (`849VCWC8+R9`). Before, these went to the geocoders, which matched them
  to some place by name or found nothing. Like decimal degrees, a query
  either reads exactly or goes to the geocoders as before.
