- Add an opt-in CET-SP camera pack: 14 intersection cameras in central São
  Paulo, the first Brazilian CCTV source. CET publishes the stills publicly but
  states no reuse terms, so the pack loads only with `CCTV_CETSP_ENABLED=1`.
  Positions come from OpenStreetMap street intersections, since CET publishes
  none, and headings are low-confidence priors. Frames are CET's thumbnail
  still, at most about 12 minutes old (vitornucci1002, #1194).
