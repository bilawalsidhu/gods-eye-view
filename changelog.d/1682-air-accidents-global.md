- New Air accidents layer (Events): about 2,500 notable aviation accidents
  worldwide from Wikidata (CC0), about 580 of them placed at their airport,
  mountain or town and marked approximate. Each has a card with the death toll,
  date, place, flight and route, aircraft and operator, linking to the
  Wikipedia article or the Aviation Safety Network record. Markers grow with
  fatalities, and a start and end year picker filters by period (IMBrandao,
  #1682).
