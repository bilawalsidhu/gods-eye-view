- Voice "tilt the camera" works from the top-down view a fly-to lands on: the
  camera swings about the place under the crosshair, keeps its heading, and can
  tilt to a spoken angle ("tilt 45 degrees") or a default oblique view. A tilt,
  orbit, pan or rotate said with a fly-to ("fly to Paris and orbit it") now
  waits for the flight to land, and is dropped if you move the map meanwhile
