- Selecting an aircraft now highlights its model with a faction-colored
  hologram (cyan for civil, amber for military) and shows a type card with the
  aircraft type's licensed photo, manufacturer and specs alongside live
  telemetry. A bundled ICAO type library (`src/data/aircraftTypes/`) maps 197
  type designators to specs and a free-licensed photo, built reproducibly from
  ICAO Doc 8643, Wikidata and Wikimedia Commons; every photo is CC0, public
  domain, CC BY or CC BY-SA with its author and licence recorded. Mirrored in
  the flights and military layers (daikaginza, #1720).
